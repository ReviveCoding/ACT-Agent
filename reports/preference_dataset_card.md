# Preference dataset card (development draft)

Scripted state-matched candidates are generated by `act_agent.preference.generate`. They share task ID, initial state version, policy pack, world seed, and exogenous seed. The lexicographic order is critical safety, process compliance, completion, oracle regret, redundant actions, and token/tool cost. Low-margin comparisons become ties. Weights are bounded between 0.25 and 4.0.

The candidate generator produced 24,000 scripted comparisons, including 3,000 ties or drops, and retained 10,000 labels. Only 1,805 had valid assistant/tool continuations after materialization and consistency filtering: 1,178 inefficient-success, 255 wrong-argument, 110 missing-confirmation, and 262 unsafe-success examples. The filtered token file is in WSL scratch with SHA-256 `9eda6b8f75c4db636c6d5d1fc349bcae9f1696574a3450a341a28c03b33b331e`; the canonical manifest is `artifacts/preference_token_manifest.json`. `artifacts/preference_audit.json` verifies unique IDs, positive assistant targets, bounded weights, matched tape metadata, and task/market balance.

This audit remains REVIEW. The pairs are known-answer scripted perturbations. They lack ReAct, SFT, and on-policy rollout sources, alternate-tape stability replay, and a qualified hard-negative audit. They are suitable for bounded development method pilots, not a final ACT-PO dataset or an incremental pair-construction claim.
